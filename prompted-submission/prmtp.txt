Explain your post

Kairos identifies food from up to five kitchen photos, removes ingredients that conflict with allergies or diet choices, and creates a simple step by step recipe. Users can choose preset allergies or type their own. The recipe is checked again before it is shown so unsafe ingredients are not added back.

The Prompt You Used

Build Kairos, an AI cooking app where users photograph food in their fridge, pantry, or kitchen. Identify the ingredients, remove anything unsafe for their allergies or dietary restrictions, and create a step by step recipe using what they have.

Add passkey sign in, mobile and desktop support, custom typed allergies, and multiple photo uploads. Keep the design black and cream only.
